# Proposal Mapping

Invoice Router is strongest when a client asks for:

- invoice or receipt extraction
- document-to-JSON workflows
- accounting-ready previews or mappings
- AI output with a human review gate
- n8n, Make, or Zapier handoff after validation

## Positioning snippets

- "I can turn invoice-like documents into structured, validated records with a review gate before downstream accounting handoff."
- "This proof repo shows approval, review, and rejection paths with replayable artifacts and no live-service risk."
- "The local bundle already includes CLI, API, export previews, and a file-backed review queue."
